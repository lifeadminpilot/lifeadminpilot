# Life Admin Pilot

We build **[Competitor Radar](https://lifeadminpilot.com/)**: one short email every Monday about what your competitors changed on their websites, what it likely means, and whether it's worth acting on.

- **Free** for up to 5 competitors. Quiet weeks are skipped.
- **Pro** covers up to 25 competitors and adds daily pricing-page checks, with an alert that shows the old and new price side by side.
- It reads competitors' public sitemaps, respects robots.txt and stays at one request per second.
