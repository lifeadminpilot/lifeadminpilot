# Life Admin Pilot

Small, focused tools for paperwork that is easy to put off.

**United States**

- [Bill Difference](https://bill.lifeadminpilot.com/) — compare two electricity bills
- [Move Quote Check](https://move.lifeadminpilot.com/) — check an interstate moving quote
- [Move Final Bill Check](https://move.lifeadminpilot.com/final-bill-check) — compare a final bill with the written estimate
- [Recoverable Depreciation](https://recoverable.lifeadminpilot.com/us/recoverable-depreciation) — track insurance depreciation
- [Pet Appeal Pilot](https://pet.lifeadminpilot.com/) — prepare a pet insurance appeal

**Germany**

- [PflegeBegleiter](https://pflege.lifeadminpilot.com/) — prepare for a care assessment
- [Gehaltsabrechnung Vergleich](https://lohn.lifeadminpilot.com/de/gehaltsabrechnung-vergleich) — compare two payslips
- [Kita-Gebührenrechner Kiel](https://kita.lifeadminpilot.com/kiel/kita-gebuehren-rechner) — daycare fees in Kiel

None of the tools require an account, and none upload documents. Where a tool relies on official figures, it cites its source — FMCSA and 49 CFR, the Texas Department of Insurance, the US Energy Information Administration, § 15 SGB XI, and the City of Kiel fee statute.
